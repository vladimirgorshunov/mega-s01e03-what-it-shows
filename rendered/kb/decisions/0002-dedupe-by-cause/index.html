0002 · Match established causes, not subject lines

owner Vladimir Gorshunov proposed reviewed 2026-11-02 · 0 days ago

Date: 2 November 2026 · Author: Vladimir Gorshunov with OpenAI Codex

Proposed replacement for: accepted decision 0001. That accepted record is preserved unchanged. This proposal does not claim support-desk adoption.

Context

The review of #4471 finds a parser/input-order problem and a booking-form hint problem inside one “known” ticket. A matching subject does not establish that the same code path failed. The supplied explorer shows both same-subject/different-cause and different-subject/same-cause cases, plus an unknown-tenant case that must not be merged merely because its text matches.

Proposed decision

Read the body and establish the affected behavior. Look up the tenant’s locale and form, and preserve the channel, original report ID and receivedAt. Use those facts and the available implementation evidence to compare causes; subject matching is only a search aid.

For this export, the shared classifier separates parser from booking-v3 hint reports. It is not an exhaustive diagnosis of production behavior. Preserve one-day/ordering observations as unresolved evidence and investigate them before claiming a new cause or its fix. Missing tenant data or an unplaced report means investigate, not “merge because both are unknown.”

Merge reports only when an established cause matches. Keep different causes in separate tickets. Deduplicate export overlap by report ID, not subject or row count. When new evidence changes a filing, move the report IDs and recompute counts; do not count the same report twice. Distinguish arrival time from the desk’s later merge time, and never use “known” to hide unexplained symptoms.

Consequences

Triage requires reading and tenant context, rather than a cheap subject-only rule. Engineering gets independent fixes with traceable counts. Uncertainty stays visible; unknown reports are not silently merged and a large count is not mistaken for one demonstrated root cause.
